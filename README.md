# FIDO Alliance and Wireless Broadband Alliance (WBA) Joint Collaboration

## Key Deliverables and Outcomes

### Drive Industry Adoption through Collaboration and Engagement

Work closely with device manufacturers, service providers, system integrators, Wi-Fi ANPs, and industrial IoT stakeholders to drive adoption and demonstrate the joint value proposition.

### Demonstrate and Validate the Technology

Deploy a proof-of-concept (PoC), as described in the [WORKFLOWS](WORKFLOWS.md), in a selected testing environment representing smart cities, enterprise IoT, industrial automation, healthcare, and/or logistics to showcase seamless Wi-Fi onboarding with OpenRoaming and FIDO Device Onboard.

### Position OpenRoaming & FIDO as the Standard for Secure, Scalable IoT Onboarding

Promote OpenRoaming + FIDO as a zero-touch, scalable, and secure solution that simplifies Wi-Fi-based IoT device onboarding while reducing IT overhead. Publish research on its benefits for security and automation.
